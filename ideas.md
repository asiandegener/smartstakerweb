# SmartStaker Reference Design Brief

## Ground-Truth Reference

The public SSPilot website at `sspilot.cc`, inspected on 15 August 2026, is the visual and structural reference. The new site must recreate its information hierarchy and interaction rhythm while replacing all SSPilot marks with **SmartStaker** and avoiding any claim of affiliation with SSPilot or Stake.

## Chosen Approach: Terminal Ledger

### Design Movement

Dark technical editorialism: a restrained product-marketing interface that blends a black terminal workspace, fine graphite construction lines, and luminous signal-green actions with expressive serif display typography.

### Core Principles

1. **Instrument-panel clarity:** product capability is organized like a precise technical readout, not a generic startup landing page.
2. **Editorial tension:** large, off-white serif headlines contrast with compact, mono-inspired metadata and UI labels.
3. **Signal over spectacle:** green is reserved for actions, status, and small active-state details; almost everything else stays charcoal, graphite, and warm white.
4. **Evidence-led density:** use framed modules, route navigation, capability lists, FAQ, and guide cards to retain the reference site’s information-rich cadence.

### Color Philosophy

The base is near-black rather than pure black so the thin grid texture remains visible. Acid chartreuse is an unmistakable SmartStaker signal color, used sparingly to make key decisions and primary actions feel deliberate. Ink, smoke, and ivory maintain a calm technical reading surface.

### Layout Paradigm

Use a wide engineering canvas with a left-anchored editorial hero, a compact operating-strip at the top, full-bleed capability panels, and offset card clusters. Avoid generic centered stacked sections; each section should feel connected by the underlying technical grid.

### Signature Elements

1. A lime outlined square-with-nodes brand mark, echoed as a faint background pattern.
2. Fine construction-grid lines and bracketed uppercase micro-labels.
3. Split headline treatments in a high-contrast serif paired with compact technical body copy.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like navigating a well-made control console: direct, quiet, and low-friction. Navigation, mobile menu, theme control, FAQ accordions, and feature cards must be keyboard accessible. Buttons that do not have a live product endpoint clearly show an in-site preview message rather than implying a real download or account action.

### Animation

Use quick, low-amplitude opacity and transform transitions: 160ms button states, 180ms mobile panel movement, and staggered hero module reveals. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and never animate large layout shifts.

### Typography System

Use **DM Mono** for utility text, tabs, metrics, and fine labels; use **DM Sans** for readable product content; use **Bodoni Moda** for display headlines. Headlines are oversized, left aligned, and use selective italic emphasis. No Inter.

### Brand Essence

**SmartStaker is a control-oriented product preview for people who want a disciplined, readable view of automated play workflows.**

Personality: analytical, lucid, composed.

### Brand Voice

Headlines use precise verbs and consequences rather than generic promise language. CTAs sound operational and transparent.

Examples:

> Configure the session before the session configures you.

> Inspect the controls. Keep the decision yours.

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark pairs a compact lime signal-mark—four stepped nodes inside a square—with a deliberately spaced uppercase **SMARTSTAKER** wordmark. The symbol is used at a visible 32–40px size in the header and as the favicon concept.

### Signature Brand Color

**Signal Lime — `#A8FF58`**

## Content and Integrity Guardrails

This is a **static marketing-site recreation**. It recreates the visible reference-site information architecture, but it does not implement gambling automation, account connections, downloads, affiliate links, claims, or payment flows. Any non-live action displays a preview notice. The footer will state that SmartStaker is an independent product preview and is not affiliated with SSPilot or Stake.
