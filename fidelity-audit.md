# SmartStaker High-Fidelity Reference Audit

## Shared site shell

The SSPilot reference uses a dense, dark landing-page system. Its desktop header has a compact green wordmark at the left, a theme control, a Stake outbound link, an aqua-to-green **Download** button, and a hamburger icon at the right. The page background is nearly black with restrained teal line texture, pale off-white serif display headlines, and muted gray body copy. Navigation on the reference is extremely compact; it does not use a multi-column product nav across the header.

## Homepage reference structure

1. Hero: thin uppercase strapline between short horizontal rules, large centered serif statement, two calls to action, a three-part metadata row.
2. Long explanatory *What is SSPilot?* section followed by four compact value cards.
3. Large product carousel/feature showcase with a dark application mockup and teal detail strokes.
4. Parallel-slot, bonus-hunt, analytics, and claimers feature sections in alternation.
5. Referral callout, horizontally scrolling originals and providers marquees, user-feedback area, and mid-page download callout.
6. SSPRF secondary-product block, numbered FAQ, latest-guides cards, and a final centered conversion panel.

## Product page reference template

The `/stake-bot` page begins with a left-aligned hero over a faint grid background. It has a small teal label, a large mixed-color serif H1, a medium-width explanatory paragraph, and a filled gradient download CTA plus outlined secondary CTA. The content sequence is: explanatory intro, concise check-mark benefits, 3-by-2 feature card grid, affiliate/signup callout, game-support grid, long-form secondary product explanation, a highlights list, four related-feature cards, FAQ, and a final CTA.

## SmartStaker parity direction

The revision will adopt the reference’s centered hero hierarchy, grid-textured product hero, familiar section order, gradient aqua/green action buttons, product carousel framing, long-form explanatory blocks, marquee grids, and final CTA. SSPilot and Stake references will be renamed to SmartStaker and neutral “supported platform” language, respectively, while preserving page structure.

## Game-page reference template

The `/stake-dice` route follows the product hero but leads with a small outlined game glyph at left, a teal categorization badge, then a large title where the game name receives the teal accent. Its page sequence is: descriptive explainer, a compact two-column parameter table, 3-by-2 feature cards, affiliate/signup CTA, a numbered four-step guide, a compact FAQ list, a related-products grid, and a final centered download CTA. The other game routes use this same structure with their own settings vocabulary, title, accent icon, and four-step workflow.

## Blog-page reference template

The `/blog` route uses the same grid-textured product hero but with a simple **BLOG** badge, large teal-accented serif title, one-line description, and a dark bordered conversion bar immediately below the intro. Article cards sit below in a spacious, single-column content rhythm rather than a dense dashboard. Each card has a compact category label, large serif headline, muted descriptive excerpt, and a small green *Read more →* link; the global header and final site footer remain unchanged.

## Legal-page reference template

The legal routes retain the grid-textured hero with a compact **LEGAL** badge, large serif title with one teal-highlighted word, and a small last-updated line. Policy content begins inside one dark, thinly bordered article surface aligned under the hero rather than in floating cards. Large white serif section titles, numbered clauses, restrained gray paragraphs, and concise bullet lists are used throughout. `/privacy` and `/terms` share this exact shell.
